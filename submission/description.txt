Namma Civic by Tri-Fusion addresses Sustainability & Social Impact: residents need an accessible way to report local problems and know whether a fix happened. It connects photo, voice and written observations with community discussion, evidence and citizen verification. Try the Firebase Hosting Spark frontend: https://leadnet-blr.web.app/. The demo shows fictional issue histories on real ward geography; new posts and connections stay in each browser. The connected source includes Gemini through Vertex AI for transcription, wording and photo observations, with schema checks, one repair and deterministic fallbacks. Code owns ward assignment, priority, transitions and metrics. The backend and live Gemini are not enabled in this deployed demo. No measured civic impact or live AI result is claimed.
