A broken footpath. An overflowing drain. A streetlight that stays dark. People share
these problems, but the post often disappears before anyone knows what happened. And
a complaint marked resolved does not always mean the problem is fixed.
We are Tri-Fusion, and this is Namma Civic: a community platform that connects
local observations, public discussion and evidence of action. Our Bengaluru prototype keeps the
issue visible from the first report to a citizen-verified outcome.
Start with New issue. A resident can take a photo, add a written
observation, or record a private voice note. With permission, the app captures a
fresh device location. The citizen reviews the description and media before sharing. The
deployed preview provides a device sandbox: posts stay in that browser. Live AI
rewriting and sharing across citizens require the connected service. The controls shown here
are captured from the actual deployed prototype.
Reporting becomes a conversation. People can react, comment and save an issue. The
People page lets citizens opt in to discovery and choose whether to connect.
In this demonstration, the profiles are fictional and requests stay in this browser.
Explore a ward to discover local records without mixing social reactions with verified
civic outcomes.
A social feed alone is not enough. Open an issue to inspect its
timeline, evidence and current status. In the connected workflow, an officer submits repair
evidence, and eligible reporting or supporting citizens confirm or dispute the claim. Shared
application rules determine whether the issue is verified or reopened. Silence never closes
it. The ledger distinguishes claimed fixes from verified outcomes. The example histories and
their totals are fictional demonstration data.
Our connected implementation is designed around Google Cloud and Firebase. Firebase Authentication identifies
sessions. The Hono API is packaged for Cloud Run and owns every mutation.
Firestore stores shared civic records, and Cloud Storage holds sanitized photos and private
voice media. Firebase Hosting is the target for serving the web application. Gemini
through Vertex AI assists with transcription, wording, issue categories, duplicate comparison and photo
observations. Each AI result is checked against a schema and task rules. One
repair is allowed, followed by a deterministic fallback. Application code computes ward assignment,
priority, service targets, verification and every public metric. This diagram describes the implemented
integrations; live cloud deployment and live model quality are not verified in this
recording.
This is a focused demonstration, not a full-scale government deployment. Next, we will
verify the connected services, involve one ward’s residents and officials, and measure real
outcomes. When outreach is configured, messages require the citizen’s final confirmation. The app
does not promise a response.
