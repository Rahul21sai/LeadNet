# Selected challenge: Sustainability & Social Impact

## Challenge description supplied by the owner

Build an AI-powered solution that addresses a meaningful environmental or societal challenge. Solutions may focus on improving resource efficiency, increasing accessibility, strengthening resilience, supporting communities, or enabling more informed and sustainable decision-making.

## Namma Civic's problem statement

Residents struggle to turn everyday civic problems into clear, actionable reports and to track whether a fix actually happened. Namma Civic connects accessible local reporting, community discussion, evidence-backed responses and citizen verification in one public record.

## Why this theme fits

- Local waste, drainage, road and accessibility problems affect shared public spaces and community wellbeing.
- Photo, voice and multilingual reporting are designed to make participation more accessible.
- Ward-based discovery and discussion connect neighbours around actionable local observations.
- Evidence and citizen verification distinguish a claimed fix from a confirmed outcome, supporting accountable decisions.

These describe the product's intended benefits. Demonstration histories and totals are fictional; no measured environmental or social impact is claimed.
